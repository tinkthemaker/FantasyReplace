## 1. Anthropic Export Crisis

The U.S. government issued a surprise export control on Anthropic's new **Fable 5** and **Mythos 5** models due to national security concerns. Because verifying user citizenship in real-time is a logistical nightmare, Anthropic completely pulled both models offline globally while they negotiate a resolution.

## 2. The Shift to "Agentic" Software

AI is moving from chatbots to autonomous web agents that complete multi-step tasks without human help. Google is rolling out an "Auto-Browse" agent to Android devices this month, OpenAI bought workflow-platform Ona to power its agents, and Adobe launched a system to help websites optimize themselves so these AI buyers don't get stuck.

## 3. Machine Traffic Dominates the Web

New Cloudflare data shows that AI crawlers and bots now account for **57.5% of all internet traffic**, officially outnumbering human users. This shift is being driven by tools like Google's "AI Mode" search, which just crossed 1 billion monthly active users as people increasingly use AI for complex planning instead of basic web browsing.

## 4. Open-Source & Enterprise Pivots

NVIDIA gave the open-source community a massive boost by releasing **Nemotron 3 Ultra**, a highly capable, permissive 550B-parameter model. Meanwhile, enterprise developer Cohere tripled its footprint in the UK to meet skyrocketing European demand for "sovereign AI"—private data infrastructure entirely independent of U.S. tech giants.
